# Image credits

Updated 11 October 2026. Seven watermark-free photographs replace all publicly displayed Sportz archive photos. Source pages and reuse licences were checked before selection. No AI images were generated and no watermarks were removed. Selected assets are served locally under `/public/images/` as optimised WebP files.

People and venues are illustrative, not affiliated with Caleb Sports Academy. Location descriptions come from source pages and do not establish each participant’s nationality or age. No model/guardian release is asserted; the academy must review personality/privacy rights and any intended promotional use before launch. Basketball and martial arts use object-only images rather than unverified athlete identities.

Images were resized without upscaling and converted to WebP. CSS uses responsive cropping and, for the hero, a readability overlay. Creative Commons adaptations are distributed under the respective source licence. The source ZIP and extracted original files remain unchanged, but unverified archive JPGs are no longer served from public/images.
