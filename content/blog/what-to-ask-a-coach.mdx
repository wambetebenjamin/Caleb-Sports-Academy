## Look beyond a list of trophies

The right coach creates an environment where children can learn, ask questions and make mistakes safely. Before enrolling, ask about experience with your child’s age group and how sessions are adapted for beginners.

## Ask about safeguarding

Find out who supervises sessions, how concerns are reported, and how the academy communicates with guardians. Ask about first-aid arrangements and the process for injuries. Clear answers matter more than impressive promises.

## Understand the development plan

A good conversation covers realistic goals, how progress is reviewed and how the coach balances competition with enjoyment. Ask how feedback is given and how parents can support progress without adding pressure.

## Meet the actual coaching team

This guide does not profile a named academy coach. Verified coach spotlights will be published only after qualifications, biographies and permissions are confirmed. A trial is a chance to meet the team in person and make an informed decision.
