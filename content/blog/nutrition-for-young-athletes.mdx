## Everyday food comes first

A varied diet helps support growth and activity. Familiar meals with a source of carbohydrate, protein, vegetables or fruit are a practical starting point. Examples might include rice or ugali with beans, eggs, fish or another suitable protein, alongside vegetables.

## Plan around training

Avoid experimenting with unfamiliar foods just before a session. A familiar snack may help when there is a long gap between a meal and practice. Individual needs vary by age, activity and health.

## Make water easy to reach

Bring a refillable water bottle. Encourage regular drinking breaks, especially in warm conditions. Coaches should make time for hydration rather than treating breaks as a reward.

## Skip the quick fixes

Young athletes do not need to chase supplement trends or restrictive diets. For allergies, special dietary needs, low energy or growth concerns, consult a qualified dietitian or healthcare professional. This article is general educational guidance, not individual medical advice.
