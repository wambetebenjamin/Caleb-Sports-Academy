## Start with consistency, not intensity

Young athletes benefit from a routine they can enjoy and sustain. Agree on a manageable schedule with your child and their coach, keeping school, sleep and family time in the picture.

## Give each session a purpose

A useful session includes a gradual warm-up, focused skill practice and time to cool down. Ask your coach what your child is working on this week. One simple goal is easier to understand than a long list of corrections.

- Pack the training bag the night before.
- Bring drinking water and suitable footwear.
- Arrive with enough time to settle in.
- Celebrate effort and improvement, not just results.

## Recovery is part of training

Rest days are not missed opportunities. If your child is unusually tired, in pain or losing enthusiasm, discuss the routine with the coach. Persistent pain should be assessed by a qualified healthcare professional.

## Keep the conversation open

After training, try asking “What did you enjoy?” before asking about performance. Sport should help children build confidence and relationships as well as skills.
