## Tell an accurate story

Start with the athlete’s sport, school, current training and recent achievements. Include dates and the names of competitions where you can verify them. Potential and commitment can matter as much as medals.

## Choose evidence thoughtfully

A short, clear video can show technique more effectively than a heavily edited highlight reel. Use a private link where possible. Do not include other children without permission, and avoid sharing home addresses, identity documents or medical records.

## Involve a parent or guardian

A guardian should review and submit an application for a minor. Keep contact details up to date so the academy can discuss assessments and next steps safely.

## Understand what is being offered

Ask what support covers, what it does not cover, how long it lasts, and whether academic or training conditions apply. Keep a written copy of any offer. An application is not a guarantee of an award, placement or overseas opportunity.

## Watch for unrealistic promises

Never pay an unverified intermediary who promises guaranteed selection. Contact the academy directly with questions about a message or offer.
